[[ats_distribution_geospatial-statistics]]
[abstract_test]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /conf/distribution/geospatial-statistics
target:: /req/distribution/geospatial-statistics
test-purpose:: Verify that geospatial statistics are present for every geometry column.
test-method::
+
--
1. For every geometry column and every row group, read the `ColumnMetaData` of the column chunk and verify that `geospatial_statistics` is present and contains `bbox` with `xmin`, `xmax`, `ymin` and `ymax`.

NOTE: Implemented by `gpio validate` check `native_geo_stats_*` and by `gpio check optimization` (factor 2, geo bbox statistics).
--
