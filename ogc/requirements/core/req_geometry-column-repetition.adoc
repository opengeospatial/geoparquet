[[req_core_geometry-column-repetition]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/core/geometry-column-repetition
part:: The repetition of a geometry column SHALL be `required` (exactly one value per row) or `optional` (zero or one value per row).
part:: A geometry column SHALL NOT be `repeated`.
====
