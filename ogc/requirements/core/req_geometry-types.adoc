[[req_core_geometry-types]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/core/geometry-types
part:: Each element of `geometry_types` SHALL be one of the strings `"Point"`, `"LineString"`, `"Polygon"`, `"MultiPoint"`, `"MultiLineString"`, `"MultiPolygon"`, `"GeometryCollection"`, optionally followed by the suffix `" Z"` (XYZ geometries), `" M"` (XYM geometries) or `" ZM"` (XYZM geometries).
part:: The elements of `geometry_types` SHALL be unique.
part:: If `geometry_types` is not empty, it SHALL contain the geometry type, including the dimension suffix, of every non-null geometry in the column.
part:: Where the column chunks of the geometry column carry `GeospatialStatistics`, the set of geometry types listed in `geometry_types` SHALL correspond to the `geospatial_types` recorded in those statistics, as specified in <<parquet-geospatial>>.
====
