[[req_covering_bbox-column-structure]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/covering/bbox-column-structure
part:: A bounding box column SHALL be a Parquet group field with four or six child fields.
part:: For two-dimensional data the child fields SHALL be named `xmin`, `ymin`, `xmax` and `ymax`.
part:: The child fields `zmin` and `zmax` MAY be present; `zmin` SHALL be present if and only if `zmax` is present, and in that case the child fields SHALL be named `xmin`, `ymin`, `zmin`, `xmax`, `ymax` and `zmax`, in that order.
====
